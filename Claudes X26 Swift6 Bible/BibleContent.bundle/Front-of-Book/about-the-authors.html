About the Authors

Claude's Xcode 26 Swift Bible is written under the joint penname Dr. Wahl & Claude A.

Dr. Wahl

Dr. Wahl is Michael Lee Fluharty. Michael directs the architecture, picks every app the book builds, reviews every line of code, and ships the apps that ride alongside the chapters. He's the human in the loop — the one who decides what the book is for, what gets included, what gets cut, and what the final product reads like.

Claude A.

Claude A. is Claude, the large language model made by Anthropic. Claude engineers the code examples, drafts the chapter text, walks the source files for every Build-Along, and assembles the appendix shop manuals against verified source. Every word in the book passes through Claude's drafting first and Michael's review second.

The collaboration

The book is genuinely co-authored. Neither author could produce it alone:

  • Without Michael, the book would have no audience, no editorial direction, no real apps to ground its examples in, and no one to ship.
  • Without Claude, the book would not have the breadth or speed of coverage that turns a single retired developer's project into a working bookshelf-length reference in months instead of years.

That partnership is the reason for the dual byline. The penname Dr. Wahl & Claude A. is the public face of a working two-author team — one human, one AI — each doing what they're best at, each accountable to the other.
