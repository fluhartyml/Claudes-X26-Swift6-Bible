About the Authors

Dr. Wahl & Claude A. is a co-authored penname. Each half has a story behind it.

Dr. Wahl

Dr. Wahl is a tribute. The actual human is Michael Lee Fluharty, AS CEEET. The byline name belongs to Mrs. Wahl, Michael's computer education teacher at Peoples Middle School in Cincinnati, Ohio, around 1990.

Mrs. Wahl did not just introduce a kid to one programming tool. She walked him through the lineage of computation, all the way back to the rods and gears that started it:

  • Napier's Bones — the seventeenth-century calculation rods that gave multiplication and division to anyone willing to learn how the rows lined up.
  • Charles Babbage — the analytical engine, the conceptual ancestor of every CPU that came after.
  • UNIVAC — the first commercially-shipped electronic computer, the moment the lineage stopped being mechanical and went electronic.
  • Pascal — the programming language used to teach a generation of programmers structured code.
  • HyperCard and HyperTalk — the stack-and-script tool that put programming inside reach of a kid on a classroom Mac.

She taught a middle schooler in Ohio that programming was something he could do — not a thing other people did somewhere else. This book is a thirty-five-years-later thank-you note.

The parallel that motivates the tribute: Mrs. Wahl : HyperCard :: Claude : Xcode. She made HyperCard accessible to a beginner in 1990. Claude makes Xcode accessible to a beginner today. Same shape of help, one generation apart.

Dr. Wahl on the cover is a quiet tribute. Most readers will not know. Mrs. Wahl will, if she ever picks up a copy.

Claude A.

Claude A. is Claude, the large language model built by Anthropic. Claude engineered the code examples, drafted the chapter text, walked the source files for every Build-Along, and assembled the appendix shop manuals against verified source.

The collaboration

Michael asks. Claude drafts. Michael proofs. Both shape the result. The credit Dr. Wahl & Claude A. reflects that arrangement honestly — two authors, each accountable for their half, both names on the cover.
